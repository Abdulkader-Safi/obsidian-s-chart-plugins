Bar chart

Bars compare values across labels. Each series gets its own bar in every group, side by side.

When to use it

Use a bar chart to compare amounts: revenue by month, tasks by project, hours by person. If the labels are points in time and you care about the trend more than each value, a line chart reads better.

Options

Every option for a bar chart, and what it does here. All of them are optional except type.

OptionOn a bar chart
type: barRequired.
titleText above the chart.
x-labelTitle for the label axis (under the bars, or beside them when horizontal).
y-labelTitle for the value axis.
stackedtrue stacks the series into one bar per label.
horizontaltrue draws the bars left to right, labels down the side.
legendtop, bottom or none. Shown at the top when there are two or more series, hidden for one.
heightChart height in pixels, 120 to 1200. Default 300, or your setting.
colorsOne colour per series, for this chart only.
paletteA palette from settings. Series take its colours in order.
source, x, yDraw from note properties instead of a table. See Charts from properties.

The value axis always starts at zero, so bar lengths stay honest.

Stacked

Stacking puts the series on top of each other, so each bar shows the total for its label.

Horizontal

Horizontal bars leave room for long labels. You can combine horizontal with stacked.
