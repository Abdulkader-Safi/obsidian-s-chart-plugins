Radar chart

A radar puts each label on its own spoke and joins each series into a shape.

When to use it

Use a radar to compare a few series across five or more measures, such as skills, product features or survey scores. Keep the measures on the same scale, or the shape misleads.

Options

OptionOn a radar chart
type: radarRequired.
titleText above the chart.
legendtop, bottom or none. Shown for two or more series.
heightChart height in pixels, 120 to 1200. The radar fits the height.
colorsOne colour per series. The fill is a see-through version of the line colour.
paletteA palette from settings.
source, x, yDraw from note properties.

stacked, horizontal, x-label and y-label have no effect. The labels on the spokes come from the first column.

How many rows

Three rows make a triangle, which says little. Five to eight rows read best.
